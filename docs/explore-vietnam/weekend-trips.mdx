# Weekend Trips

One of the biggest advantages of living in Hanoi is how easy it is to explore the rest of Vietnam. Whether you're looking for mountains, beaches, cultural experiences or adventure, there are countless destinations within a few hours of the city.

---

## Popular Weekend Destinations

### Sa Pa

Famous for mountain scenery, rice terraces and trekking.

**Travel Time:** 5–6 hours

---

### Ninh Bình

Known as the "Halong Bay on Land" with stunning limestone mountains and river boat tours.

**Travel Time:** 2–2.5 hours

---

### Hạ Long Bay

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its limestone islands and overnight cruises.

**Travel Time:** 2.5–3 hours

---

### Mai Châu

A peaceful valley with beautiful scenery, cycling and traditional villages.

**Travel Time:** 3–4 hours

---

### Cát Bà Island

A great alternative to Hạ Long Bay with beaches, hiking and boat tours.

**Travel Time:** 3–4 hours

---

### Pu Luong Nature Reserve

Perfect for hiking, rice terraces and relaxing away from the city.

**Travel Time:** 4–5 hours

---

## Tips

- Book transport early during public holidays.
- Carry cash when visiting smaller towns.
- Ninh Bình is one of the easiest and most rewarding weekend trips for first-time visitors to Vietnam.
