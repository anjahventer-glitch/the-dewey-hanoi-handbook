# Northern Vietnam

Northern Vietnam is known for its dramatic mountains, rice terraces, winding mountain roads and rich cultural diversity. Living in Hanoi gives you easy access to some of the country's most spectacular destinations.

---

## Must-Visit Destinations

### Sa Pa

- Rice terraces
- Trekking
- Fansipan Mountain
- Ethnic minority villages

**Travel Time:** 5–6 hours

---

### Hà Giang

Vietnam's most famous motorbike route.

Highlights:

- Hà Giang Loop
- Mountain passes
- Traditional villages
- Incredible scenery

Best visited with a guided tour if you are not an experienced rider.

---

### Ninh Bình

Often called "Ha Long Bay on Land."

Highlights:

- Tam Cốc
- Tràng An
- Múa Cave
- Bái Đính Pagoda

---

### Hạ Long Bay

UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for limestone islands and overnight cruises.

---

### Pu Luong Nature Reserve

Ideal for:

- Hiking
- Nature
- Rice terraces
- Relaxing weekends

---

### Mai Châu

A peaceful valley perfect for cycling, hiking and experiencing rural Vietnam.

---

## Best Time to Visit

- **September – November:** Cool weather and beautiful scenery.
- **March – April:** Pleasant temperatures and green landscapes.

---

## Tips

- Bring a light jacket, even in summer—mountain evenings can be cool.
- Weather changes quickly in the mountains.
- Book accommodation in advance during public holidays.
- Consider travelling on Friday afternoon and returning Sunday evening to maximise your weekend.
