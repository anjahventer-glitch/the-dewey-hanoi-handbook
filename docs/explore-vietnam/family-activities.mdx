# Family Activities in Hanoi

Hanoi is a fantastic city for families. From indoor play cafés to parks, museums and animal attractions, there are plenty of activities to keep children entertained throughout the year.

---

## Family-Friendly Cafés

### Lalina Kids Café

A popular café with a dedicated indoor play area, making it a favourite for families with young children.

---

### Blue Blau

A stylish family café with a safe play space for children and excellent coffee and food.

---

### Bubble House

A popular indoor play café where children can explore while parents enjoy a coffee or meal.

---

## Parks & Outdoor Activities

### 🌳 Hanoi Botanical Garden

A peaceful green space perfect for:

- Family walks
- Picnics
- Feeding squirrels
- Letting young children explore

---

### Hoàn Kiếm Lake

One of Hanoi's most vibrant public spaces.

At weekends the surrounding streets become pedestrian-only and often feature:

- Street performances
- Traditional games
- Live music
- Family activities

---

## Animals & Nature

### Hanoi Zoo (Thu Le Park)

A popular destination for younger children featuring:

- Zoo animals
- Playgrounds
- Paddle boats
- Open green spaces

---

### VinKE & Vinpearl Aquarium

Located inside Times City.

Highlights:

- Large aquarium
- Interactive educational exhibits
- Indoor play area

Perfect for rainy days.

---

## Museums

Family favourites include:

- Vietnam Museum of Ethnology
- Vietnam Museum of Natural History
- Hanoi Museum

Many museums have interactive exhibits suitable for children.

---

## Indoor Activities

- Jump Arena Trampoline Park
- TiNiWorld
- KidZooona
- Ice Skating
- Bowling
- Cinema

Great options during hot summers or rainy weekends.

---

## Seasonal Activities

Throughout the year Hanoi hosts:

- Christmas markets
- Mid-Autumn Festival celebrations
- Tết flower markets
- Weekend festivals
- Lantern displays
- Outdoor concerts

---

## Tips

- Visit outdoor attractions early in the morning during summer.
- Many shopping malls have excellent children's play areas.
- Carry snacks, water and sunscreen when visiting parks.
- Follow local family Facebook groups for upcoming events and activities.

> 💡 **Tip:** Hanoi has a very active family and expat community. It's easy to find playgroups, weekend events and family outings through local Facebook groups and school communities.
