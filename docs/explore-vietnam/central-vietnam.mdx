# Central Vietnam

Central Vietnam is famous for its beautiful beaches, historic cities, caves and relaxed atmosphere. It is an excellent destination for long weekends and school holidays.

---

## Must-Visit Destinations

### Hội An

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its lantern-lit streets, tailor shops and riverside cafés.

Highlights:

- Ancient Town
- Night Market
- Japanese Covered Bridge
- An Bang Beach
- Tailor-made clothing

---

### Đà Nẵng

A modern coastal city with beautiful beaches and excellent restaurants.

Highlights:

- Mỹ Khê Beach
- Dragon Bridge
- Marble Mountains
- Sơn Trà Peninsula
- Ba Na Hills & the Golden Bridge

---

### Huế

Vietnam's former imperial capital.

Highlights:

- Imperial City
- Royal Tombs
- Perfume River
- Traditional Vietnamese cuisine

---

### Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng National Park

One of the world's most impressive cave systems.

Highlights:

- Paradise Cave
- Phong Nha Cave
- Dark Cave
- Son Doong (expedition only)

---

## Best Time to Visit

**February August**

Expect warm temperatures and plenty of sunshine.

## Getting There

Most people fly from Hanoi to:

- Đà Nẵng
- Huế

Domestic flights are affordable and take approximately **1 hour 20 minutes**.

---

## Tips

- Hội An is especially beautiful at night when the lanterns are lit.
- Ba Na Hills is popular—arrive early to avoid crowds.
- If visiting Huế, consider hiring a driver to explore the Imperial Tombs.
- Domestic flights are often inexpensive if booked in advance.
- A combination of Đà Nẵng and Hội An makes one of the best long-weekend trips in Vietnam.
