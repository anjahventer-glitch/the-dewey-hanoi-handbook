# Southern Vietnam

Southern Vietnam offers a completely different experience to the north, with tropical islands, bustling cities and the famous Mekong Delta. The warm climate makes it an excellent destination throughout the year.

---

## Must-Visit Destinations

### Ho Chi Minh City

Vietnam's largest city, known for its vibrant food scene, nightlife and historical landmarks.

Highlights:

- Ben Thanh Market
- War Remnants Museum
- Notre-Dame Cathedral
- Nguyen Hue Walking Street
- Café Apartments

---

### Phú Quốc

Vietnam's largest island and one of the country's top beach destinations.

Highlights:

- White sand beaches
- Snorkelling & diving
- Sunset Town
- VinWonders
- Safari Park
- Night Market

Perfect for families and relaxing holidays.

---

### Mekong Delta

Explore Vietnam's famous waterways and floating markets.

Highlights:

- Boat tours
- Local villages
- Floating markets
- Tropical fruit farms

---

### Côn Đảo Islands

A quieter island destination known for its beautiful beaches, marine life and history.

Ideal for:

- Diving
- Snorkelling
- Relaxation
- Nature lovers

---

## Best Time to Visit

Southern Vietnam has warm weather year-round.

The dry season (November–April) is generally the best time to visit.

---

## Getting There

Most visitors fly from Hanoi to:

- Ho Chi Minh City
- Phú Quốc
- Côn Đảo (usually via Ho Chi Minh City)

Flight time is approximately **2 hours**.

---

## Tips

- Phú Quốc is one of the easiest tropical beach holidays from Hanoi.
- Ho Chi Minh City has a faster pace than Hanoi—allow extra travel time during peak hours.
- Book flights early during school holidays for the best prices.
- If you're looking for a relaxing beach holiday, Phú Quốc is one of the best destinations in Vietnam and a favourite among many Dewey staff.
