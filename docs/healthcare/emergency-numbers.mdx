# Emergency Numbers

Hopefully you will never need these numbers, but it is a good idea to save them in your phone when you first arrive in Vietnam.

---

## Emergency Services

| Service | Number |
|---------|:------:|
| 🚑 Ambulance | **115** |
| 🚒 Fire | **114** |
| 👮 Police | **113** |

---

## Tips

- Save your nearest hospital in Google Maps.
- Keep your a copy of your passport and insurance card on your phone.
- Save your emergency contacts in your phone.
- Share your location with a trusted colleague or family member if travelling alone while unwell.
- If the situation is not life-threatening, many expats prefer travelling directly to an international or recommended private hospital rather than calling an ambulance.
