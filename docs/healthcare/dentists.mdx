# Dentists

Hanoi has many excellent dental clinics offering routine check-ups, emergency treatment and cosmetic dentistry. Most clinics popular with the expat community have English-speaking staff.

---

## Recommended Dentists

### Westcoast International Dental Clinic

One of the most popular dental clinics among expatriates, offering general, cosmetic and specialist dentistry.

**Website:** https://westcoastinternational.com

---

### Serenity International Dental Clinic

Modern dental clinic with English-speaking dentists and a wide range of dental services.

**Website:** https://serenitydentalclinic.vn

---

### Australian Dental Clinic

A long-established international clinic offering comprehensive dental care.

**Website:** https://australiandentalclinic.vn

---

## Services

Most clinics provide:

- Routine check-ups
- Professional cleaning
- Fillings
- Root canal treatment
- Crowns and bridges
- Teeth whitening
- Orthodontics (braces & Invisalign)
- Dental implants

---

## Tips

- Book appointments in advance.
- Ask your insurance provider whether dental treatment is covered.
- Many clinics accept international insurance or can provide documentation for reimbursement.
- Dental care in Vietnam is generally of a very high standard and is often significantly more affordable than in many other countries.
