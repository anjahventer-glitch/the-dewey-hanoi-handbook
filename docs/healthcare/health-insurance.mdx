# Health Insurance

As a Dewey employee, your health insurance will be arranged by the school. It is important to understand what your policy covers and which hospitals and clinics are included in your network.

---

## Before Your First Appointment

Make sure you know:

- Your insurance provider (BoaViet)
- Your policy number
- Which hospitals offer direct billing
- Whether pre-authorisation is required for specialist treatment

If you are unsure, contact the HR Department before your appointment.

---

## Direct Billing

Many international hospitals in Hanoi offer direct billing with major insurance providers. This means the hospital can claim directly from your insurer, reducing or eliminating upfront payment.

Always confirm this with the hospital before your appointment.


---

## Reimbursements

If direct billing is not available:

- Keep all receipts and invoices.
- Request a medical report if required.
- Submit your claim according to your insurance provider's process.
- Sometimes you might be covered by social insurance.

---

## Emergency Treatment

In an emergency:

1. Go to the nearest suitable hospital.
2. Contact HR as soon as possible.
3. Keep all medical documentation and receipts.

---

## Helpful Tips

- Save a copy of your insurance card on your phone.
- Carry your passport or another form of identification when visiting a hospital.
- Check your policy before arranging elective procedures or specialist appointments.
