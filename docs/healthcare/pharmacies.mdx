# Pharmacies

Pharmacies are easy to find throughout Hanoi and are well stocked with prescription and over-the-counter medication. Many pharmacists can assist in English, particularly in areas popular with expats.

---

## Recommended Pharmacy Chains

### Long Châu Pharmacy

One of Vietnam's largest and most trusted pharmacy chains with locations across Hanoi.

**Website:** https://nhathuoclongchau.com.vn

---

### An Khang Pharmacy

A modern pharmacy chain offering prescription medication, health products and personal care items.

**Website:** https://www.nhathuocankhang.com

---

### Pharmacity

A convenient nationwide pharmacy chain with many branches throughout Hanoi.

**Website:** https://www.pharmacity.vn

---

### Syrena Shopping Centre Pharmacy

Located on the **second floor of Syrena Shopping Centre**, next to **WinMart**.

This pharmacy is a favourite among the expat community because it stocks a wider range of **imported medicines, vitamins and international brands** than many other pharmacies in Hanoi.

**Location:**  
Syrena Shopping Centre  
51 Xuân Diệu, Tây Hồ, Hanoi

---

## Prescriptions

- Many common medications can be purchased without a prescription.
- Prescription medication from your home country may require a Vietnamese prescription.
