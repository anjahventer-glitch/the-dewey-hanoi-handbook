# Hospitals

Hanoi has an excellent healthcare system with a range of international and private hospitals. The hospitals below are commonly used by Dewey staff and the expat community.

---

## International Hospitals

### Vinmec International Hospital

One of Vietnam's leading private hospitals with modern facilities, English-speaking staff and a wide range of specialist services.

**Website:** https://www.vinmec.com

---

### Family Medical Practice

A popular choice for expatriates offering general practice, paediatrics, vaccinations and specialist referrals.

**Website:** https://www.vietnammedicalpractice.com

---

### Hanoi French Hospital (HFH)

The first international hospital in Hanoi, offering comprehensive medical care with internationally trained doctors and multilingual staff. It is well regarded for specialist care, surgery and maternity services. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

**Website:** https://www.hfh.com.vn/en/

---

## Popular Local Private Hospitals

### Thu Cuc International General Hospital

A modern private hospital in Tây Hồ offering a wide range of medical and specialist services. English interpreters are available for international patients.

**Website:** https://benhvienthucuc.vn

---

### MEDLATEC Hospital

Well known for health screenings, laboratory testing and diagnostic services. A popular choice for routine healthcare.

**Website:** https://medlatec.vn

---

### Hong Ngoc General Hospital

A highly regarded private hospital with several campuses across Hanoi, offering comprehensive medical and specialist services.

**Website:** https://hongngochospital.vn

## Tips

- Keep your passport and health insurance details with you when visiting a hospital.
- Save the location of your nearest hospital in Google Maps.
- Insurance providers may not cover the cost of tests that return a negative result, even if they were requested by a doctor.
- It's a good idea to check your cover and keep all medical receipts and reports for claims.
- It's common to visit a hospital rather than a family doctor (GP), even for minor illnesses or injuries.
