## How to Use the Map

The map is organised into different cuisine categories using **layers**. Click the **Layers** button in the top left corner of the map to show or hide different types of restaurants, such as Italian, Vietnamese or Breakfast & Brunch.

Click on any restaurant pin to view its name, location and additional information. You can also zoom in, search nearby areas and open the location directly in Google Maps for directions.
