# Food Delivery

Food delivery in Hanoi is fast, affordable and widely used. Whether you're ordering lunch at school or dinner at home, you'll have hundreds of restaurants to choose from.

---

## GrabFood

The most popular food delivery platform in Vietnam.

You can order:

- 🍕 Restaurant meals
- ☕ Coffee
- 🥤 Bubble tea
- 🍰 Desserts
- 🛒 Groceries (GrabMart)

Website:
https://www.grab.com/vn/

## Payment Tip

Once you have a Vietnamese bank account or an international card that works in Vietnam, **link it to your Grab account**.

Paying by card significantly increases the chances of a driver accepting your booking, especially for **GrabFood** orders. For cash orders, drivers often have to pay for your food upfront before collecting the money from you, and many prefer to avoid this. Prepaid orders are generally accepted much more quickly.

---

## ShopeeFood

Another excellent delivery app with a huge selection of local restaurants and regular discounts.

Website:
https://shopeefood.vn

---

## Typical Delivery Time

- 20–45 minutes
- Longer during heavy rain or peak meal times

---

## Payment

Most delivery apps accept:

- Cash
- Bank card
- Vietnamese bank QR payments

---

## Tips

- Connect your bank card for better driver acceptance
- Check restaurant ratings before ordering.
- Delivery fees are usually very reasonable.
- Tipping isn't expected but is appreciated during bad weather.
- Keep your phone nearby so the driver can contact you if needed.
