# Must Try Vietnamese Food

One of the best parts of living in Vietnam is experiencing the incredible local food. Don't be afraid to step outside your comfort zone—you'll quickly discover why Vietnamese cuisine is loved around the world.

---

## 🍜 Phở

Vietnam's most famous noodle soup, traditionally served with beef or chicken.

**Recommended:** Phở Khôi Hói, Phở Thìn

---

## 🥖 Bánh Mì

A crispy Vietnamese baguette filled with meat, vegetables and fresh herbs.

Perfect for breakfast or lunch.

---

## 🍖 Bún Chả

Grilled pork served with rice noodles, fresh herbs and dipping sauce.

A Hanoi speciality and a must-try.

**Recommended:** Bún Chả Hương Liên (Obama Bun Cha)

---

## 🍲 Bún Bò Huế

A rich, spicy beef noodle soup from central Vietnam.

Perfect if you enjoy stronger flavours.

---

## 🍗 Cơm Tấm

Broken rice served with grilled pork, egg and pickled vegetables.

A popular everyday meal.

---

## 🥟 Nem Rán

Vietnamese fried spring rolls.

Crispy, delicious and perfect for sharing.

---

## ☕ Vietnamese Coffee

Don't leave Vietnam without trying:

- 🥚 Egg Coffee
- 🥥 Coconut Coffee
- 🇻🇳 Traditional Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Cà Phê Sữa Đá)

---

## Tips

- Busy restaurants are usually a good sign.
- Carry cash when visiting smaller local restaurants.
- Don't be afraid to ask colleagues for recommendations.
- Some of the best meals in Hanoi come from small family-run restaurants.
