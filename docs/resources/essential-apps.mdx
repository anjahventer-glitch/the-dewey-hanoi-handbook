# Essential Apps

Download these apps during your first week in Hanoi. They'll make everyday life, work and settling in much easier.

---

## Edufit Staff

The official staff app used by The Dewey Schools for attendance and staff communication.

---

## Tasky B

Used by Edufit schools for maintenance requests, IT support and other internal tasks.

---

## BaoViet Direct

Manage your BaoViet health insurance, access your digital insurance card and submit claims.

---

## VssID

The official Vietnam Social Security app for viewing your social insurance information and contributions.

---

## Grab

Vietnam's all-in-one app for transport, food delivery, grocery shopping and parcel delivery.

---

## Lalamove

A convenient delivery app for moving furniture, transporting large items and arranging same-day courier services across Hanoi.

---

## Zalo

Vietnam's most popular messaging app, used by landlords, businesses, schools and many local services.

---

## BIDV SmartBanking

Manage your Vietnamese bank account, transfer money, make QR payments and pay bills.

---

## Shopee

Vietnam's largest online marketplace for almost anything you need.

---

## Lazada

Another popular online shopping platform with fast delivery across Hanoi.

---

## IQAir

Monitor Hanoi's air quality, especially during the winter months.

---

## VPN

Useful for accessing certain websites and improving online security. Popular options include NordVPN, ExpressVPN and Surfshark.

---

## Google Maps

Essential for navigation, discovering new places and getting around Hanoi.

---

## Google Translate

Translate conversations, menus and signs instantly.
