# Markets

Local markets are a great way to experience everyday life in Hanoi. They're perfect for buying fresh produce, flowers, clothing, household goods and unique Vietnamese products.

---

## Quảng An Flower Market

### Why Visit

Hanoi's largest flower market and one of the city's most beautiful early-morning experiences.

### You'll Find

- Fresh flowers
- Indoor & outdoor plants
- Photography opportunities
- Seasonal displays

📍 **Location:** https://maps.google.com/?q=Quang+An+Flower+Market+Hanoi

---

## Đồng Xuân Market

### Why Visit

Hanoi's largest traditional market and a great place to experience the city's bustling atmosphere.

### You'll Find

- Clothing
- Souvenirs
- Household goods
- Local food
- Wholesale products

📍 **Location:** https://maps.google.com/?q=Dong+Xuan+Market+Hanoi

---

## Chợ Hôm Market

### Why Visit

A popular neighbourhood market used by locals for everyday shopping.

### You'll Find

- Fresh fruit & vegetables
- Meat
- Seafood
- Clothing
- Fabrics

📍 **Location:** https://maps.google.com/?q=Cho+Hom+Market+Hanoi

---

## Quảng Bá Flower Market

### Why Visit

A convenient local market popular with residents living around Tây Hồ.

### You'll Find

- Flowers
- Fresh fruit
- Vegetables
- Herbs

📍 **Location:** https://maps.google.com/?q=Quang+Ba+Market+Hanoi

---

## Tips

- Carry cash (small notes are helpful).
- Bring your own shopping bag.
- Visit early for the freshest produce.
- Bargaining is common for clothing and souvenirs, but not for food.
- Even if you don't buy anything, visiting a local market is one of the best ways to experience Vietnamese culture.
