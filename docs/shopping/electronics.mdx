# Electronics

Whether you need a new laptop, kitchen appliance, phone or charger, Hanoi has plenty of reliable electronics retailers. Most stores offer delivery, installation and warranty services.

---

## MediaMart

One of Hanoi's most popular electronics retailers offering a wide range of products at competitive prices.

You'll find:

- Laptops
- Mobile phones
- TVs
- Kitchen appliances
- Washing machines
- Air conditioners
- Small household appliances

**Website:** https://mediamart.vn

---

## Điện Máy Xanh

Vietnam's largest electronics and appliance retailer with stores throughout Hanoi.

Popular for:

- Mobile phones
- Computers
- Home appliances
- Kitchen equipment
- Accessories
- Repairs and warranties

**Website:** https://www.dienmayxanh.com

---

## Phone & Computer Repairs

### ProCare (Tây Hồ)

A trusted repair shop recommended by many members of the Hanoi expat community. They specialise in Apple products, laptops, mobile phones and general computer repairs.

Services include:

- Apple repairs
- Laptop repairs
- Mobile phone repairs
- Battery replacements
- Screen replacements
- Data recovery
- Computer servicing

**Website:** https://www.facebook.com/procarefans

**Location:** Tây Hồ, Hanoi
