# Furniture & Home

Whether you're furnishing a new apartment or simply picking up a few household essentials, Hanoi has plenty of excellent options to suit every budget.

---

## BAYA

One of Vietnam's most popular home and furniture stores offering modern furniture, décor and household accessories.

You'll find:

- Sofas
- Dining tables
- Beds
- Home décor
- Kitchenware
- Storage solutions

**Website:** https://baya.vn

---

## JYSK

Affordable Scandinavian-inspired furniture and home accessories.

Great for:

- Bedding
- Towels
- Kitchenware
- Office furniture
- Storage
- Home décor

**Website:** https://jysk.vn

---

## MM Mega Market

A favourite for new arrivals setting up a home.

You'll find:

- Kitchen appliances
- Cleaning supplies
- Cookware
- Storage containers
- Household essentials
- Bulk groceries

**Website:** https://mmvietnam.com

---

## Muji

Ideal for minimalist furniture, stationery and quality household items.

**Website:** https://www.muji.com/vn

---

## IKEA

Although Vietnam doesn't currently have an official IKEA store, many local businesses import IKEA products and deliver them within Hanoi.

Search for:

- IKEA Hanoi
- IKEA Vietnam

on Facebook or Google.

---

## Tips

- Shopee is excellent for smaller household items.
- MM Mega Market is ideal for stocking up on kitchen essentials.
- BAYA and JYSK are great starting points when furnishing an apartment.
- Ask colleagues if they're selling furniture before buying new—many departing teachers sell quality items at excellent prices.
