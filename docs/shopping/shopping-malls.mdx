# Shopping Malls

Hanoi has a variety of modern shopping malls offering everything from groceries and clothing to cinemas, restaurants and entertainment.

---

## Lotte Mall West Lake

One of Hanoi's newest and largest shopping malls.

Popular for:

- International fashion brands
- Restaurants and cafés
- Lotte Mart supermarket
- Cinema
- Family entertainment

**Website:** https://lottemallwestlakehanoi.com.vn

---

## AEON Mall Long Biên

A popular destination for shopping, groceries and family outings.

Highlights:

- AEON Supermarket
- Clothing
- Homeware
- Food court
- Entertainment

**Website:** https://www.aeon.com.vn

---

## Vincom Mega Mall Royal City

One of Hanoi's largest underground shopping centres.

Highlights:

- International brands
- Ice skating rink
- Cinema
- Restaurants

**Website:** https://vincom.com.vn

---

## Vincom Center Metropolis

A convenient shopping mall in Ba Đình.

- International brands
- Arcade
- Cinema
- Restaurants

**Website:** https://vincom.com.vn

---

## Syrena Shopping Centre

A favourite among West Lake residents.

Highlights:

- WinMart
- Imported groceries
- Pharmacy
- Restaurants
- Everyday essentials

Ideal if you live in Tây Hồ.

---

## Tips

- Most malls have underground parking.
- Credit cards are widely accepted.
- Shopping malls are excellent places to escape the summer heat.
