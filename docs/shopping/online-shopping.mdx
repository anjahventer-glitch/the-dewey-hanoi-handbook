# Online Shopping

Online shopping is incredibly popular in Vietnam and is often the quickest and cheapest way to buy almost anything. Most deliveries arrive within 1–3 days, with many arriving the same day.

---

## Shopee

Vietnam's largest online shopping platform.

**Website:** https://shopee.vn

---

## Lazada

A trusted online marketplace offering a wide range of local and international products.

**Website:** https://www.lazada.vn

---

## Tiki

Known for fast delivery and genuine products, particularly books, electronics and household items.

**Website:** https://tiki.vn

---

## Facebook Marketplace

A great place to buy and sell second-hand furniture, appliances and household goods. Many expats also use it when moving to or leaving Hanoi.

---

## Payment

Most online stores accept:

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Bank Cards
- Vietnamese Bank Transfers
- Digital Wallets

---

## Delivery

Most deliveries arrive within:

- Same day
- Next day
- 2–3 days

Delivery tracking is available through each app.

---

## Tips

- Read seller reviews before ordering.
- Check product ratings and photos.
- Cash on Delivery is a great option if you're unsure about a seller.
- Shopee is usually the best place to start when looking for almost anything.
