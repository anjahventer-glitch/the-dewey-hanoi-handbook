# Accommodation

Finding suitable accommodation is one of the first priorities when moving to Hanoi. There are many options available, from serviced apartments to private rentals, and choosing the right area can make a big difference to your daily commute and lifestyle.

---

## Popular Areas

Many Dewey staff choose to live in one of the following areas:

- **Tây Hồ (West Lake)** – Popular with expatriates, cafés, restaurants and lakeside living.
- **Ciputra** – Secure residential community with many international families.
- **Ngoại Giao Đoàn (Diplomatic Area)** – Modern apartments close to The Dewey Schools THT Campus.
- **Starlake** – New residential area within easy reach of the school.

---

## Rental Costs

Typical monthly rental prices (excluding utilities):

| Apartment | Typical Cost |
|------------|-------------:|
| Studio | 7–12 million VND |
| 1 Bedroom | 10–18 million VND |
| 2 Bedroom | 15–30 million VND |
| 3 Bedroom | 25+ million VND |

> Prices vary depending on location, furnishing and building facilities.

---

## Common Inclusions

Many apartments include:

- Wi-Fi
- Weekly cleaning
- Water
- Building security
- Parking
- Gym or swimming pool (some buildings)
