# Utilities

Most apartments in Hanoi require tenants to pay for utilities separately. Always confirm what is included in your rental agreement before signing a lease.

---

## Electricity

- Usually charged monthly.
- Typical cost: **800,000–2,500,000 VND/month**, depending on apartment size and air-conditioning use.
- Air conditioning is the biggest contributor to electricity costs.

> ⚠️ Ask whether you pay the government rate or a landlord's fixed rate.

---

## Water

- Often included in the rent.
- If charged separately, it is generally inexpensive.

Where to buy

- Order directly from the official brand or a trusted delivery service.
- Ask your apartment reception or landlord—they almost always know the local delivery company.
- You can also find bottles at large supermarkets like LOTTE Mart or WinMart, but delivery is much easier.

Typical cost
- 60,000–75,000 VND per 19L bottle.
- The first order may require a refundable deposit for the bottle.
- Most suppliers deliver to your apartment door, and many offer same-day or next-day delivery.

---

## Gas

Most apartments use electric cooktops. If your apartment uses gas, ask the landlord how refills are arranged.

---

## Rubbish Collection

Usually included in the building management fee.
