# Household Shopping

Shopping in Hanoi is easy, with everything from local markets to large supermarkets and international grocery stores.

---

## 🥬 Fresh Produce

Many residents buy their fresh fruit and vegetables from local street vendors or neighbourhood markets.

Fresh produce in Vietnam is often locally grown, seasonal and less processed, so it doesn't last as long as supermarket produce in some countries. It's best to buy fruit and vegetables every few days rather than stocking up for the week.

You'll find most of the fruit and vegetables you're familiar with, along with many delicious tropical varieties to try.

---

## 🛒 Supermarkets

For everyday essentials, these supermarkets can be found throughout Hanoi:

- WinMart
- GO! Hypermarket
- Lotte Mart
- MM Mega Market

These stores stock groceries, cleaning products, toiletries and basic household items.

---

## 🌍 International Grocery Stores

If you're looking for imported products from home, these stores are excellent options.

### Annam Gourmet

Premium international supermarket with products from Europe, Australia and the USA.

📍 https://maps.google.com/?q=Annam+Gourmet+Hanoi

---

### L's Place

Popular with expats for imported groceries and international brands.

📍 https://maps.google.com/?q=L's+Place+Hanoi

---

### Sedona Mini Mart

A favourite in Tây Hồ with a wide range of American, Japanese and Korean products.

📍 https://maps.google.com/?q=Sedona+Mini+Mart+Hanoi

---

### Hưng Long (Xuân Diệu)

Well known for American groceries, toiletries and imported household products.

📍 https://maps.google.com/?q=Hung+Long+Xuan+Dieu+Hanoi

---

### K-Market

A Korean supermarket chain with groceries, snacks, frozen food and household items.

📍 https://maps.google.com/?q=K+Market+Hanoi

---

## 🛍️ Online Shopping

Almost anything can be delivered to your door.

- [Shopee](https://shopee.vn)
- [Lazada](https://www.lazada.vn)

---

## 💡 Tips

- Buy fresh fruit and vegetables every few days for the best quality.
- Local markets are usually the cheapest option for fresh produce.
- WinMart is ideal for everyday shopping.
- Annam Gourmet and L's Place are excellent for imported products.
- Shopee is often the cheapest place to buy household items online.
