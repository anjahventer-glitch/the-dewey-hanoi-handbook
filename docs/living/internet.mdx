# Internet

Vietnam has fast and affordable fibre internet. Most apartments can be connected within a few days.


---
## Recommended Providers

### FPT Telecom

- Reliable fibre internet
- English support available in some branches
- Popular with expatriates

Website:
https://fpt.vn :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

### Viettel

- Excellent nationwide coverage
- Competitive pricing
- Good customer support

Website:
https://viettel.vn

---

### VNPT

- Reliable service
- Good value packages
- Wide coverage throughout Hanoi

Website:
https://vnpt.vn :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Typical Monthly Cost

| Speed | Price |
|-------|------:|
| 150–300 Mbps | 200,000–350,000 VND |
| 500 Mbps+ | 350,000–500,000 VND |

## Tips

- Ask your landlord which provider already services the building.
- Many apartments already have internet installed.
