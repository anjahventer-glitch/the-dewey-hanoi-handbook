# Money & Banking

Setting up your finances is one of the first things you should do after arriving in Vietnam. Most businesses accept card payments, but cash is still widely used for markets, small shops and local restaurants.

---

## Currency

Vietnam uses the **Vietnamese Dong (VND)**.

It is helpful to become familiar with common notes:

- 10,000 VND
- 20,000 VND
- 50,000 VND
- 100,000 VND
- 200,000 VND
- 500,000 VND

---

## Opening a Bank Account

Dewey will advise you on opening a Vietnamese bank account for salary payments.

Common banks include:

- Vietcombank
- BIDV
- Techcombank
- ACB

---

## Mobile Banking

Most banks offer excellent mobile banking apps with:

- Instant transfers
- QR code payments
- Bill payments
- Savings accounts

---

## Cash & ATMs

ATMs are easy to find throughout Hanoi.

Many banks charge withdrawal fees for cards issued by other banks.

---

## Cashless Payments

QR payments are extremely common in Vietnam.

Many restaurants, cafés and shops accept QR payments through Vietnamese banking apps.

---

## Tips

- Keep a small amount of cash with you for taxis, markets and street food.
- Notify your home bank before travelling to Vietnam.
- Download your Vietnamese bank's mobile app as soon as your account is activated.
- Keep some emergency cash at home in case your bank card is temporarily unavailable.
