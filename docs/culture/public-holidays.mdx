# Public Holidays

Vietnam celebrates a number of public holidays throughout the year. During these periods, schools, banks, government offices and many businesses may close or operate with reduced hours.

---

## Major Public Holidays

### New Year's Day
**1 January**

A national public holiday celebrating the start of the new year.

---

### Tết (Vietnamese Lunar New Year)

The most important holiday in Vietnam.

Tết usually falls between **late January and mid-February** and celebrations often last for around a week.

During this time:

- Schools close.
- Many businesses close.
- Restaurants and shops may have limited opening hours.
- Transport and flights become extremely busy.

---

### Hung Kings' Festival

Celebrated in honour of Vietnam's founding kings.

---

### Reunification Day

**30 April**

Commemorates the reunification of Vietnam.

---

### International Workers' Day

**1 May**

A national public holiday.

---

### National Day

**2 September**

One of Vietnam's largest national celebrations, often marked by fireworks, events and ceremonies.

---

## Planning Ahead

During major holidays:

- Book flights well in advance.
- Reserve accommodation early if travelling.
- Expect heavier traffic than usual.
- Check opening hours before visiting attractions or government offices.

---

## Tips

- Tết is a wonderful opportunity to experience Vietnamese culture.
- Many local restaurants and family-run businesses close during Tết, so plan meals ahead.
- Large supermarkets usually remain open for at least part of the holiday period.
- If you're staying in Hanoi during Tết, enjoy the quieter streets—it's one of the few times of year when the city becomes noticeably less busy.
