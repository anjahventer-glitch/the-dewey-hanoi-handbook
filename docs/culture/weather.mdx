# Weather

Hanoi experiences four distinct seasons, each with its own charm and challenges.

---

## Spring (March – April)

- Mild temperatures
- Increasing humidity
- Pleasant weather for exploring the city

---

## Summer (May – August)

- Hot and humid
- Temperatures often exceed **35°C**
- Frequent afternoon thunderstorms
- UV levels can be very high
- Typhoon Season

Stay hydrated and carry an umbrella during this time of year.

---

## Autumn (September – November)

Many people consider autumn to be the most beautiful season in Hanoi.

- Cooler temperatures
- Lower humidity
- Clear skies
- Comfortable weather for outdoor activities

---

## Winter (December – February)

Winter is generally cool and can feel surprisingly cold because most homes do not have central heating.

Typical temperatures range from **10–20°C**, although colder days do occur.

Bring:

- A warm jacket
- Closed shoes
- Layers for cooler mornings and evenings

---

## Air Pollution

Hanoi experiences its poorest air quality during the cooler months, typically **November to March**, with **December and January** often being the worst.

Poor air quality is caused by a combination of factors, including heavy traffic, construction dust, industrial emissions, seasonal agricultural burning and weather conditions that trap pollution close to the ground.

Most days are perfectly manageable, but it's worth checking the air quality before planning long periods outdoors.

## Protecting Yourself

If the air quality is poor:

- Limit prolonged outdoor exercise.
- Keep windows closed where possible.
- Consider using an air purifier at home.
- Wear a quality **N95 mask** if you will be outside for extended periods.

## Tips

- Air quality changes daily, so it's worth checking the AQI each morning.
- Parks and lakes are often slightly better than busy main roads.
- Avoid exercising alongside major roads during periods of high pollution.
- Keep a reusable N95 mask in your bag during the winter months.

---

## Rainy Season

Heavy rain is common during summer.

Always carry:

- A compact umbrella
- A lightweight rain jacket

Roads can flood quickly after heavy storms, so allow extra travel time.
