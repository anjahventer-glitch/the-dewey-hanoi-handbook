# Language

The official language of Vietnam is **Vietnamese**. While many people working in tourism, international schools and larger businesses speak English, learning a few basic Vietnamese phrases will make everyday life much easier.

---

## Useful Apps

- **Google Translate** – Instant translation for text, voice and conversations.
  https://translate.google.com

- **Google Lens** – Translate menus, signs and documents using your phone's camera.
  https://lens.google

- **Zalo** – Vietnam's most popular messaging app. Many businesses and service providers use Zalo for communication.
  https://zalo.me

## Essential Phrases

| English | Vietnamese | Pronunciation |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| Hello | Xin chào | Sin Chow |
| Thank you | Cảm ơn | Gahm Un |
| Yes | Vâng | Vung |
| No | Không | Khom |
| Excuse me | Xin lỗi | Sin Loy |
| How much? | Bao nhiêu? | Bao Nyew? |
| Goodbye | Tạm biệt | Tahm Bee-et |

---

## Tips

- A smile goes a long way.
- Most younger Vietnamese people speak at least some English.
- Don't worry about perfect pronunciation—people appreciate the effort.
- As a general rule, younger people are often addressed as **em** (for both men and women), while older people are commonly addressed as **anh** (older male) or **chị** (older female). Using these terms is a polite sign of respect.
