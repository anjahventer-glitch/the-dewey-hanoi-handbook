# Grab

Grab is Vietnam's all-in-one transport and delivery app. It's one of the first apps you'll use after arriving in Hanoi and is essential for getting around the city.

---

## Grab Services

- 🚗 GrabCar – Car rides
- 🛵 GrabBike – Motorbike taxis
- 🍜 GrabFood – Restaurant delivery
- 🛒 GrabMart – Groceries and convenience stores
- 📦 GrabExpress – Parcel delivery

---

## Why Use Grab?

- Upfront pricing before you book.
- Cash or card payment.
- Live driver tracking.
- In-app messaging and translation.
- Safer than negotiating taxi fares.

---

## Payment Tips

Once you have a Vietnamese bank account or an international card that works in Vietnam, **link it to your Grab account**.

Paying by card significantly increases the chances of a driver accepting your booking, especially for **GrabFood** orders. For cash orders, drivers often have to pay for your food upfront before collecting the money from you, and many prefer to avoid this. Prepaid orders are generally accepted much more quickly.

---

## Weather Tips

During heavy rain, demand for Grab increases dramatically and it can become difficult to find a driver.

If rain is forecast, consider travelling earlier or allowing extra time for your journey.

## Tips

- GrabBike is usually the fastest way to travel during peak traffic.
- Double-check your pickup location before booking.
- Save your home and work addresses in the app.
- Link your payment card as soon as possible for faster bookings and food deliveries.
- Check the weather before travelling, as rain can make it much harder to book a Grab.
