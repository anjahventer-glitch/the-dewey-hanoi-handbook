# Taxis

While most people use Grab, traditional taxis are still widely available throughout Hanoi. Always choose a reputable company to avoid overcharging.

---

## Recommended Taxi Companies

### Mai Linh Taxi

One of Vietnam's largest and most trusted taxi companies.

Website:
https://mailinh.vn

---

### G7 Taxi

A reliable taxi service operating throughout Hanoi.

Website:
https://g7taxi.vn

---

### Xanh SM

An all-electric taxi service with a modern app and fixed pricing.

Website:
https://www.xanhsm.com

---

## Payment

Most taxis accept:

- Cash
- Credit/Debit Cards
- QR Payments (some vehicles)

Always confirm your preferred payment method before the journey begins.

---

## Tips

- Grab is usually the easiest option because the fare is shown before booking.
- Avoid unmarked taxis waiting outside tourist attractions.
- Save your home address in Google Maps to show the driver if needed.
- Xanh SM is becoming increasingly popular in Hanoi. Their electric vehicles are clean, quiet and competitively priced.
