# Hanoi Metro

The Hanoi Metro is a clean, affordable and reliable way to travel around parts of the city. While the network is still expanding, it is becoming an increasingly useful option for commuting and exploring Hanoi.

---

## Current Lines

The metro currently serves several key areas of Hanoi and continues to expand.

---

## Fares

- Approximately **8,000–15,000 VND** per trip.
- Tickets can be purchased at station ticket machines.

## Operating Hours

---

Approximately:

- **05:30 – 22:30** daily

(Check the official website for the latest timetable.)

---

## Advantages

- Air-conditioned trains.
- Avoids road traffic.
- Inexpensive.
- Easy to use.

---

## Tips

- Google Maps provides accurate metro directions.
- Keep a small amount of cash available for ticket purchases if needed.
- The metro is ideal for weekend trips into the city centre.
