# Buses

Hanoi has an extensive public bus network covering most parts of the city. Buses are an affordable way to travel, although journey times can be longer during peak traffic.

---

## Fares

- Standard fare: **8,000–15,000 VND**
- Pay in cash when boarding (small notes are helpful).

---

## Advantages

- Very affordable.
- Extensive network.
- Air-conditioned buses on most routes.

---

## Helpful Apps

### BusMap

BusMap provides live bus tracking, routes and estimated arrival times.

Website:
https://busmap.vn

### Google Maps

Google Maps provides accurate public transport directions throughout Hanoi.

https://maps.google.com

---

## Tips

- Use BusMap to plan your journey before leaving.
- Keep small cash available for tickets.
- During rush hour, buses can be very busy.
- If you're unsure where to get off, show the driver or conductor your destination on Google Maps.
- BusMap is the easiest way for newcomers to navigate Hanoi's bus network.
