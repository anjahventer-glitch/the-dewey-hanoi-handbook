# Walking

Walking is a great way to explore Hanoi, particularly around West Lake, the Old Quarter and local neighbourhoods. However, it can be quite different from walking in many other countries.

---

## What to Expect

- Pavements (sidewalks) are often uneven.
- Motorbikes may be parked on footpaths.
- Street vendors sometimes occupy pedestrian areas.
- Crossing roads can feel intimidating at first.

---

## Crossing the Road

The best approach is to:

- Walk at a steady pace.
- Avoid sudden stops or changes of direction.
- Make eye contact with approaching drivers where possible.
- Allow traffic to move around you.

> ⚠️ Never run across the road or suddenly change direction.

---

## Tips

- Carry tissues and hand sanitiser.
- Wear comfortable shoes.
- Stay aware of motorbikes using footpaths.
- Reflective clothing is recommended if walking after dark.
